Features

Everything You Need for Seamless Handovers

From AI-powered document analysis to human review workflows and professional PDF reports.

AI Knowledge Extraction

Google Gemini analyses your documents and extracts decisions, tasks, risks and dependencies automatically.

Human Review & Approval

AI is never trusted blindly. Every extracted item goes through mandatory human review before becoming verified knowledge.

Risk & Dependency Detection

Automatically identifies missing owners, unresolved decisions, timeline conflicts and critical dependencies.

Professional Reports

Generate beautiful PDF handover reports with one click. Download CSV for tasks, print or share online.

Acknowledgement Tracking

Share reports with incoming team members and track who has acknowledged receiving the handover information.

Role-Based Access Control

Admin, Project Manager and Team Member roles with backend-enforced permissions and full audit trails.

Audio/Video Meeting Ingestion

Direct upload of MP4/MP3 recordings (Zoom/Google Meet exit interviews). Multimodal AI transcribes and captures unwritten context automatically.

Semantic Search & RAG Graph

Vector database search allows natural-language queries across historical handovers to recover past decisions, architecture rationale, and domain experts.

GitHub · Jira · Slack Sync

1-click push pending tasks and risks into Jira issues or GitHub tickets, and dispatch instant handover sign-off alerts directly into Slack channels.

How KnowBridge AI Works

1

Create Your Project

Set up a project, invite team members, assign roles and configure the workspace.

2

Upload Documents

Drag and drop PDFs, Word docs, CSVs or text files. Text is extracted automatically.

3

AI Analyses Everything

Gemini AI reads your documents and extracts decisions, tasks, risks and dependencies into structured data.

4

Human Review & Approve

Project managers review every AI-extracted item. Approve accurate items, reject incorrect ones, add context.

5

Generate Handover Report

One click generates a professional PDF report containing only approved, verified information.

6

Share & Acknowledge

Share the report with incoming team members who acknowledge receipt. Full audit trail maintained.

The Knowledge-Loss Problem

Every time a team member leaves a project, critical knowledge walks out the door. The cost is real, measurable, and avoidable.

Scattered Documents

Key project information lives in dozens of files, emails, and chats—impossible to find when you need it.

Lost Context

The "why" behind decisions, rejected alternatives, and historical context vanish when people leave.

Repeated Mistakes

Without knowledge of past failures, new teams re-make the same costly mistakes that were already solved.

Delayed Projects

Onboarding takes months instead of weeks. New team members struggle to get up to speed without context.
